Two Doors, One Rhythm

The calendar is the map. This is the doorway.

They are not duplicates. Each part of Ancient Rhythm has its own purpose.

The Calendar

The complete year: all 13 moons, their dates, equinoxes, solstices, Days Out of Time, and the larger pattern of time. It answers, “When?”

This Companion

A living daily experience: today’s moon, its wisdom, a ritual, a garden teaching, and a guide who can answer gently. It answers, “How do I live within this cycle?”

The Journal

The private, written practice: reflections, intentions, patterns, releases, and discoveries across the year. It answers, “What is changing within me?”

Ancient Rhythm moons are 28-day sacred calendar cycles anchored to the equinoxes. They are not presented as exact astronomical moon-phase predictions.
